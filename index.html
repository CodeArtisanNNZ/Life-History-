Before the human story

Angels, jinn & the first act of refusal

Islam describes creations that natural science is not equipped to date or test. So this opening chapter belongs to the revelation track, with no invented prehistoric year attached to it.

Islamic sources

Angels are not the rebels in this story

Authentic hadith describes angels as created from light. The Qur'an describes angels as obeying Allah's commands. It is therefore inaccurate to describe the angels themselves as having a rebellious nature.

Created from lightSahih Muslim 2996
They do not disobey AllahQur'an 66:6

Islamic sources

Jinn existed before Adam; Iblis was one of them

The Qur'an says the jinn were created before humanity from scorching fire. When commanded with the angels to prostrate to Adam, Iblis refused; Qur'an 18:50 explicitly identifies him as being from the jinn.

Jinn created beforeQur'an 15:27
Iblis was from the jinnQur'an 18:50
No date given. The Qur'an and authentic hadith do not provide a measurable geological date for the creation of angels or jinn.
02
Natural history

A planet forms beside a young Sun

Earth did not break off from the Sun. Modern planetary science says the Sun and planets formed from the same collapsing cloud of gas and dust, with Earth assembling about 4.54 billion years ago.

Scientific evidence

Solar nebula → planet

About 4.6 billion years ago a dense interstellar cloud collapsed into a spinning disk. Most mass gathered into the Sun; material farther out collided and accreted into planets. Radiometric evidence gives Earth an age of about 4.54 billion years.

Solar system~4.6 billion years ago · NASA
Earth~4.54 billion years old · USGS
Islamic sources

Creation belongs to Allah

The Qur'an repeatedly directs attention to the heavens and Earth as created signs. It does not give a modern astrophysical recipe for planet formation, so the page does not turn verses into claims about nebular physics.

Method matters: “Who created?” and “what physical process can we measure?” are not treated as interchangeable questions here.

Biological history

Life begins simple, then diversifies

The oldest uncontested fossils are about 3.5 billion years old. Science can investigate traces of early life and mechanisms of evolution; the exact first transition from chemistry to biology remains an active research problem.

~3.5 billion years ago

Uncontested fossil evidence of early life

Single-celled organisms dominated Earth for an immense span of time.

Complex multicellular animals expand

Paleozoic worlds

Marine animals diversify, vertebrates expand, plants and animals colonize land, and the era ends in the largest known mass extinction.

Mesozoic — the dinosaur era

Non-avian dinosaurs dominate many terrestrial ecosystems while mammals also exist and birds arise from theropod dinosaurs.

04
Human origins

Hominins in the fossil record — Adam in revelation

This is the most important boundary on the site. Fossils document populations and biological relationships. Revelation tells the story of Adam. The Qur'an does not give Adam a fossil age or explicitly place him relative to Neanderthals, so this page does not manufacture that connection.

7–6 million years ago

Sahelanthropus

One of the oldest known species in the hominin family tree; fossils are known from Chad.

3.85–2.95 million years ago

Australopithecus afarensis

Lucy's species; a well-known bipedal hominin from eastern Africa.

1.89 million–110,000 years ago

Homo erectus

A long-lived Homo species with human-like body proportions and wide geographic dispersal.

~400,000–40,000 years ago

Neanderthals

Close relatives of modern humans. Genetic evidence shows interbreeding with ancestors of many living non-African populations.

Ancient DNA lineage

Denisovans

Known initially through DNA and limited fossils; closely related to Neanderthals.

~300,000 years ago–present

Homo sapiens

Our living species; the oldest widely accepted fossils are from Africa and date to roughly 300,000 years ago.

Adam in Islamic sources

Created from clay; honored with knowledge

“I am going to place a successive authority on earth.”

Qur'an 2:30 — short excerpt; see 2:30–39, 7:11–25, 15:26–44, 20:115–123, 38:71–85.

The Qur'an describes Adam's creation, the command to prostrate, Iblis's refusal, life in the Garden, the error, repentance, and descent to Earth.

Scientific boundary

No scientific date for “Adam” can be extracted from scripture

Science can date fossils and reconstruct biological ancestry. It cannot identify a named prophetic individual from Qur'anic revelation. Likewise, the Qur'an does not say whether Neanderthals lived before, during or after Adam.

Do not force an alignment
Adam & his spouse

Hawwa / Eve

The Qur'an repeatedly refers to Adam's spouse but does not name her “Hawwa” in the Qur'anic text. “Hawwa” is the standard name in Islamic tradition. Qur'an 4:1 speaks of humanity being created from a single soul and its mate.

The Garden, temptation and repentanceQur'an 2:35–39; 7:19–25; 20:117–123
The children of Adam

The first murder

Qur'an 5:27–31 tells the account of two sons of Adam, one killing the other after an offering was accepted from one and not the other. The Qur'an itself does not name them Qabil and Habil, so this page keeps the Qur'anic wording.

Prophetic history

From Nuh to ʿIsa — without invented dates

The Qur'an gives rich narratives but usually not calendar dates. Common internet BC timelines often come from later Biblical chronologies; because this project is intentionally Islamic-source-first, those dates are not imported here.
